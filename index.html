Material Provenance & Engineering

Uncompromising integrity in every layer.

We meticulously curate materials that withstand Bangalore's seasonal humidity shifts and daily wear, partnering directly with certified sustainable forestry and European hardware pioneers.

  • Seasoned Natural Hardwoods: Burma Teak, American Walnut & European White Oak hand-finished with low-VOC oils.
  • IS 710 Marine-Grade BWP Plywood: Calibrated boiling waterproof cores for absolute stability in wet areas and modular kitchens.
  • Acoustic Slatted Panelling: Precision-fluted timber louvers with recycled PET acoustic felt for serene room dynamics.
  • Architectural Metalwork: Matte powder-coated structural steel framing, concealed soft-close Blum hinges, and satin brass details.
  • Natural Stone & Quartz Surfaces: Book-matched Italian Statuario marble and stain-resistant quartz for high-traffic surfaces.
